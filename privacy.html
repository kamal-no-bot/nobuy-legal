Privacy Policy

The short version: this app collects nothing, because it sends nothing anywhere.

What is collected

Nothing. There is no account, no sign in, no analytics, no advertising identifier and no crash reporting service.

No bank, no card, no financial connection

This is the part worth saying plainly, because most apps in this category do the opposite. No Buy has no bank connection, no card linking, no open banking provider, no statement import and no third party financial service of any kind. It never sees a real transaction, a balance, an account number or a card. Every figure in it is one you typed.

What stays on your device

  • Your rules, and when you changed them.
  • Every day you recorded, and whether you kept to your rules on it.
  • The temptations you logged, with the amount, the category and any note you wrote.
  • Whether you have bought the unlock.

All of it is stored in the app's own container on your iPhone, plus a small shared file that lets the widget draw your month on the home screen. That shared file carries totals and day states, never the name of anything you bought and never a note. None of it is transmitted, and the developer cannot see any of it.

Network

The app contains no networking code. The only network activity is Apple handling a purchase or a restore if you choose to buy, which is Apple receiving your purchase rather than this app sending anything to the developer.

Sorting done on your device

When the app suggests a category for something you logged, the suggestion is produced by Apple's on-device model or, where that is unavailable, from a list bundled inside the app. What you typed is not sent anywhere for this, and the suggestion is a starting point you can change.

Notifications

If you turn on a reminder, it is scheduled on your device by iOS. No reminder is sent from a server, and their contents never leave your iPhone.

Share cards

Share cards are drawn on your device and deliberately carry aggregates only: they never contain the name of a purchase, a note, or the detail of a slip. Where a card goes after you share it is decided by you and by whatever app you send it to.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. The app never sees your payment details. Apple gives the app a receipt confirming what you own, and that stays on your device.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes everything above. There is no copy anywhere else, so there is nothing to ask the developer to erase.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the change appears here and in the app release notes. A version of this app that started sending data anywhere, or that connected to a bank, would be a different app to the one described here.
