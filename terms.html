What No Buy is

No Buy is a private diary of a no-spend challenge you set for yourself. You write your own rules, you record each day, and you note the things you resisted and the things you bought anyway. Everything the app shows is arithmetic over what you entered by hand.

What No Buy is not

  • It is not financial, investment, budgeting, debt or tax advice.
  • It is not a budget, a forecast or a plan for your money.
  • It does not connect to any bank, card, wallet or financial institution, and it never reads a real transaction.
  • It does not know your income, your obligations or your circumstances.
  • It is not a substitute for advice from a qualified financial adviser.

The money figures in the app are your own estimates of what you chose not to spend. They are a record of your intentions, not a measurement of your accounts, and nothing in the app should be relied on as a statement of your financial position.

Patterns are associations, never causes

Where the app points out that something tends to happen together, it is describing a pattern in what you typed. It is not telling you why, it is not predicting what you will do, and it is not advising you to change anything.

Your record is yours

The app never deletes your history, never rewrites a past day, and never shows a day you did not record as a day you kept. A slip is logged as a slip and resets nothing. This is a rule the app is built around rather than a promise about intentions.
