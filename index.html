Common questions

Does it connect to my bank?

No, and it never will. There is no bank connection, no card linking, no open banking, no import of statements and no networking code of any kind in the app. Everything in your ledger is there because you typed it.

I slipped and bought something. Have I ruined the month?

No. A slip is logged as a slip and the month continues. Nothing is reset, nothing is deleted, and no screen tells you that you have failed. The record simply shows what happened, which is the only thing a record is for.

Why is a day I did not fill in shown as unknown instead of a good day?

Because the app does not know. A day you did not record is a day nobody recorded, and colouring it in would be inventing a fact about your life to make a grid look better. Fill it in whenever you like and it becomes a real day.

Can I change my rules partway through?

Yes, and the change applies from the day you make it. It never rewrites the days before it, because a rule that applies backwards makes the whole record meaningless.

Does it need internet?

No. The app contains no networking code at all. The only network activity is Apple handling a purchase or a restore if you choose to buy.

Do I need an account?

There is no account, no sign in and no email to give. That also means there is no sync between devices in this version, and your ledger lives on the iPhone you keep it on.

What stays free?

Your rules, your daily check in, your ledger, the current month and your share cards. Those are the app, and they are not behind a purchase.

How do I cancel or restore a purchase?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. To restore a purchase on a new device, open Settings inside the app and tap Restore Purchase.
